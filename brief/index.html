SAP halts most travel and hiring, blaming AI's soaring cost

Aug 8 · 68 pts · 44 comments

SAP suspended most corporate travel and hiring last month because of AI's rising costs, according to an internal email obtained by 404 Media; only AI-related travel and hires are exempt. A current employee says the freeze is still in effect even as SAP rolls out a new AI tool company-wide, which they expect will push costs higher still.

“In other words, AI is now driving their budget decisions.” — jqpabc123 on HN

Message your other Claude Code sessions

Aug 8 · 137 pts · 61 comments

Claude Code sessions can now deliver messages to each other directly — one session can warn another about a breaking change or hand off a finding via new ListAgents/SendMessage tools (v2.1.224+, macOS/Linux). Inbound messages go through approval rules, and a receiving session is barred from treating a message as consent for anything it wasn't already permitted to do.

“I've used this feature and saw some weird messages: > hold swarm, I prepare safe exfil” — dist-epoch on HN

Gentoo's Bugzilla goes dark after AI scraper overload

Aug 8 · 166 pts · 109 comments

Gentoo developer Michał Górny took the project's Bugzilla offline, saying it had become unusable under load from LLM scrapers hitting it from thousands of rotating IPv4 addresses with no pattern he could block. He added he isn't looking for mitigation tips — as an unpaid volunteer, not a sysadmin, he doesn't have time to fight the traffic.

“OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and others are normally fairly well behaved (Minus Anthropic attempting to hide behind a browser-for-hire company).” — mrweasel on HN

ChatGPT starts blocking direct requests to copy an author's style

Jul 2026 · 63 pts · 49 comments

ChatGPT now refuses prompts asking it to write "in the exact style" of named authors — living (Stephen King, J.K. Rowling) or dead — offering instead to capture their "broad qualities" in its own voice. Ars Technica notes the shift could matter legally, as OpenAI continues fighting copyright suits from authors over training data.
