Anthropic warns infostealer malware is hijacking Claude sessions to drain usage

Aug 30

Anthropic is emailing affected users that common infostealer malware (Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine, and others) on their own PCs stole already-authenticated Claude session cookies, letting attackers replay the session and burn through usage limits without a password or 2FA. Anthropic is signing out affected accounts, removing saved payment methods, and refunding unauthorized charges, and stresses the malware isn't related to Claude itself.

DeepSeek nears $7.4 billion funding round at $74 billion valuation ahead of 2027 IPO

Aug 29

DeepSeek is close to a roughly 50 billion yuan ($7.4B) round at a 500 billion yuan ($74B) pre-money valuation, per the Wall Street Journal and SCMP, positioning it for a Shanghai Star Market listing as soon as 2027. Backers reportedly include CATL, with Tencent, CPE, and Legend Capital in talks; proceeds would fund roughly 1 gigawatt of additional compute as founder Liang Wenfeng shifts the lab from hedge-fund-backed to externally capitalized.

We tested how AI chatbots would handle foreign propaganda. They did surprisingly well

Aug 30

NPR and NewsGuard posed 30 questions built on false narratives spread by China, Iran, and Russia to ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, and Claude. The chatbots mostly pushed back on the falsehoods and outperformed traditional search engines, though AI-generated summaries at the top of search results fared worse than the chatbots did.
