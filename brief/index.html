Anthropic releases FlashProve for provable inference

Nov 2, 2026

Anthropic released an open-source research prototype that uses zkSNARKs to prove that a committed model produced a response without revealing its weights. On a quantized Llama 4 Maverick test, it reports roughly 110 proved tokens per second on one H100, while noting a substantial overhead versus ordinary inference.
