How Claude's text watermarking works

Aug 14

Anthropic is embedding an invisible watermark in Claude's text output by subtly influencing which word Claude picks among equally viable options — "the watermark only changes the source of the randomness used to pick among words," per the post. The pattern is detectable only with a special key, doesn't affect output quality, and rolls out globally starting this month to comply with EU AI Act disclosure requirements.
