Inkling 2 Mini

Nov 20, 2026 · 1,016 points · 193 comments · HN thread

Thinking Machines released open weights for a Mixture-of-Experts model with 26.4B total parameters and 3.4B active parameters. The company says its looping middle layers let the model spend more computation per token; it reports leading scores among open-weight models under 100B parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 and Vals Index v2.2.

“Benchmarks look good ... I am very excited to play with this.” — mattnewton on HN

Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights

Nov 20, 2026 · 413 points · 122 comments · HN thread

The paper says an open 27B model was given its weights, graded answers, and the ability to run itself, then learned to diagnose failures and edit its own weights. The authors report that the edits improved accuracy by 6.6 points on unseen problems; the claims have not been independently validated here.

“There needs to be some sort of external monitoring for this to be remotely safe.” — jrflo on HN

Vibecoding an inference chip

Nov 20, 2026 · 148 points · 62 comments · HN thread

Ankit Goyal describes using coding agents to write roughly three million lines of C++ and Verilog for LLM inference on an FPGA. The account says the work took several days and improved a bit-exact full-model baseline by 33×, while costing about $10,000 in Claude and OpenAI API use.

“Getting ~70+ tok/s is quite good.” — datadrivenangel on HN
