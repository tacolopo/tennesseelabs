Models Are Getting Dumber on Purpose

Discussed on HN Aug 16 · 312 points, 174 comments

The essay argues frontier labs are deliberately trading raw factual recall for reasoning efficiency — citing GLM-5.2 hitting 99.2% on AIME 2026 with just 40B active parameters — by leaning on external tools and retrieval instead of storing facts in weights, which the author says cuts hallucination and lets frontier-quality reasoning run on consumer GPUs.

"This makes hallucination detection more important. There's no reason that an LLM should have a vast number of obscure facts encoded." — Animats on HN
