OpenAI cuts developer pricing for frontier GPT-5.6 Sol model by more than 20%

Aug 21

OpenAI is cutting GPT-5.6 Sol API prices to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens (from $5 and $30) for three months, through at least Nov. 21, citing growing competition from Anthropic and Chinese AI models. The cuts apply to the API and eligible ChatGPT Work/Codex credit plans; Pro, Plus, and Business subscription pricing is unchanged.

Tesla, Uber, and Waymo all get the OK to operate thousands of robotaxis in Nevada

Aug 20

The Nevada Transportation Authority unanimously approved permits letting Tesla, Uber, and Waymo run paid robotaxi service in Clark County (Las Vegas), authorizing up to 8,000 vehicles combined over the next year — 5,000 for Tesla, 1,000 for Waymo, and 1,000 for Uber (via Motional and Zoox). Company reps said actual fleets will likely land well below the caps; local taxi and livery groups opposed the approvals over oversaturation concerns.
