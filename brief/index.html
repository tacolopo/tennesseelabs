Oct 25, 2026 · 163 points · 57 comments on HN

How Cerebras deploys trillion-parameter models

Oct 22, 2026 · 160 points · 56 comments on HN

Cerebras explains how it maps a 3-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 example across WSE-3T wafers, assuming four-bit weights and an eight-bit KV cache. It says the design targets 1,000–2,000-plus tokens per second while requests stream through a pipeline.

“It’s describing a different type of parallelism — pipeline parallelism.” — bubblethink on HN

Why AI-generated language can be hard to understand

Oct 21, 2026 · 199 points · 169 comments on HN

The authors find that nine frontier and near-frontier models use more low-frequency word sequences and inanimate subjects with animate verbs than human writing. They argue that optimization for information density and reasoning can work against easy human communication.
