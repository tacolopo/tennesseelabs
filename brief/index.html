Google releases Cogentic papers on integer factorization, discrete logs and more

HN thread posted Nov 25, 2026 · 494 points · 357 comments · HN thread

Google's repository lists Cogentic research papers dated Nov. 25 on integer factorization, discrete logarithms, approximate Nash equilibria, log-rank, and related topics. The HN thread treated the collection as evidence of a multi-agent proof-discovery workflow reaching publishable research artifacts.

"Cogentic appears to be a specialized harness for existing LLMs to collaborate together to solve mathematical conjectures, using a central orchestrator and a shared database of intermediate proofs." — david-gpu on HN

Much shorter version of GRH Lean verification from Anthropic

HN thread posted Nov 25, 2026 · 463 points · 244 comments · HN thread

The repository provides Lean 4 proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis and generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions, based on Levent Alpöge's work with Claude. It offers a roughly 300,000-line readable edition and a 76,000-line compact edition; the project says machine checks have passed but no mathematician has read the proof end to end.

"From ~17M lines to just ~76k lines, a 99.56% reduction." — bananaflag on HN

Bare Metal Qwen

HN thread posted Nov 25, 2026 · 156 points · 39 comments · HN thread

This project boots Qwen3-0.6B directly on x86-64 hardware or KVM without a general-purpose operating system, C, Rust, or libc. Its README reports a 24 KB assembly kernel, persistent KV cache, and token-exact parity with its earlier implementation.

"Pretty cool as a concept, reminds me of the self-booting disks from back in the day." — hankbond on HN

Opus 5.5 writes its Magnum Opus (2500 times)

HN thread posted Nov 25, 2026 · 55 points · 67 comments · HN thread

Dan Price documented a month-long experiment in which Claude Opus 5.5 wrote, rendered, and filmed a 25-minute orchestral work through about 2,500 review rounds. The write-up estimates roughly 35 billion Opus tokens and stores the score sources, reviews, and scripts in a public repository.
